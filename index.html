iViraam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: everything you enter in iViraam — your name, details, habit, plan and logs — stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers and can't see it. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data as described below. Subscribing to iViraam Ad-Free turns those ads off.

Who we are

iViraam is made by Manish Maurya ("we"). Contact: iviraam.app@gmail.com.

Information you enter in the app

iViraam asks for optional details such as your first name, year of birth, gender, occupation, the tobacco or nicotine product you use, how much you use and what it costs. It also records your plan, the slots you use or carry over, and any unplanned use you log.

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to any third party, including advertisers. It is used only to run the app: building your schedule, reminders, savings figures and the Health tab. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

iViraam Ad-Free subscription

You can remove all ads with an optional monthly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. While you are subscribed, the app does not load Google AdMob, does not show its own quit-support ads, and does not ask for ad consent or tracking permission. You can cancel any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Advertising

iViraam is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, and interactions with ads.

Ad requests include general topics (such as quitting smoking, health and wellness) and any ad interests you choose yourself in About you. They never include your name, age, gender, occupation, the product you use or anything you log.

You can decline tracking in the prompt or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Users under 18 receive age-appropriate ad treatment and are not asked to allow tracking.

iViraam also shows its own quit-support messages (quitlines and stop-smoking aids). These are part of the app and don't collect any data.

Notifications

Slot reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to a server.

Your choices

  • Every personal detail in the app is optional.
  • Edit your details any time in Settings → About you.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Reset app, or by deleting the app.
  • Control ad tracking in iOS Settings and in the app's ad privacy choices.

Children

iViraam is not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 see only quitlines and counselling in the app's quit-support messages, and receive age-appropriate ads.

Health information

The Health tab gives general information from public health sources. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or quitline about your own health.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
